The SafeCypher position
Not reduce. Eliminate.

Why "eliminate" matters

Every fraud strategy in the market today is a reduction strategy — ML scoring, 3DS challenges, step-up auth. They all assume some fraction will get through and try to minimise it.

SafeCypher is different. A dynamic code that expires in seconds can't be stolen, replayed, or phished. The attack surface isn't minimised; it ceases to exist.
