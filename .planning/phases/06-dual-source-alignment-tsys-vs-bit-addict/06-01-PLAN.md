(b) `supabase/migrations/0028_v_alignment_daily.sql`, three objects.

  1. `alignment_status(p_tsys bigint, p_bit_addict bigint, p_tolerance int, p_settled boolean, p_coverage_complete boolean) returns text`, `language sql`, `immutable`, `security invoker`, `set search_path = public`. Its `case` branches IN THIS ORDER, and the order is the point of the function: incomplete coverage first and unconditionally, returning the needs-review literal; then absolute difference within tolerance, returning the aligned literal; then settled, returning the mismatch literal; else the needs-review literal. The header comment must state in full that this order is the deliberate INVERSE of `v_reconciliation_billing_daily` / `v_reconciliation_inventory_daily`, which check equality first, and that copying their order here would let a day with two uncovered sources that both happen to read zero render as aligned — cite D-12 by name.
