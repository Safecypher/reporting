<objective>
Close the two gaps 05-VERIFICATION.md recorded as `status: failed`, and nothing else.

Gap 1 (blocker): `/reconciliation`'s enrolled/unenrolled summary figures became period-scoped when
05-02 added the outer period predicate to `v_reconciliation_inventory_daily`, but their labels still
