Land the two facts every later plan in this phase depends on, and land them live.

Fact one is the **lease**: a per-file claim column on `ingested_files` plus the conditional-UPDATE
claim function that makes "two triggers, one file" safe by construction, and the single TypeScript

deployed Netlify site. D-05 made this measurement a condition of the phase, because the answer
decides whether plan 13-05 builds chained-attempt convergence or deletes the idea.

ceiling and you carry complexity that was never needed. Measuring first costs one deploy.

probe; and a recorded ladder of measurements that plan 13-05's decision checkpoint consumes.

    - **10 attempts.** The ceiling on self-chained continuations in 13-05. Ten attempts at a bounded
