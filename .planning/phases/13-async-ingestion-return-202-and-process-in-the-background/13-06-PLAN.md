<objective>
Give the system the trigger that does not depend on anyone's browser, and give a file that never
finishes somewhere to be seen.

D-01 chose both triggers deliberately: the client fire for a watching user's immediate progress, and
the daily drain sweep for everything else — a closed tab, a dropped request, a continuation that ran
out of attempts. D-03 then required that a `pending` row which never completes be surfaced, because
this phase is what makes `pending` routine and five such rows already sat unnoticed for up to three
days.

Purpose: ROADMAP SC-3 and SC-4. Convergence that does not depend on a browser, and loudness when
convergence does not happen.

Output: a bounded sweep inside the one existing daily job, a `stuckPending` group in the grouped
Slack alert, and the stuck count on the `alert_runs` evidence row.
</objective>
