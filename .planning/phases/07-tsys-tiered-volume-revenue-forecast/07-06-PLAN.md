<objective>
Deliver the phase's headline surface: `/revenue` shows the actual-to-date figure beside a
projected month-end or year-end figure with its low/high band and a permanent method caption, and
the revenue chart extends forward with a visually distinct dashed segment.

Purpose: ROADMAP SC1 (FCST-01) — the actual/projected distinction must be unmistakable at a
glance — and SC5 (FCST-05) — below the threshold the card says so rather than printing a wild
figure.

Output: a projected KPI card, a `projected` series on `RevenueChart`, projection-aware
re-bucketing, and the `/revenue` page wiring that gates all of it on D-12.

**User story (surfaced, not invented):** see 07-01-PLAN.md §objective — the ROADMAP Phase 7 Goal
line is not in `As a / I want to / so that` form and no story has been invented in its place.
</objective>

<planner_notes>
**Why the chart needs a per-day projected series rather than a single month-end point.** The chart
plots revenue per bucket, not a cumulative total, so a forward segment needs a per-day projected
revenue value. `revenue_forecast_daily_for_period` (plan 07-04) supplies exactly that, computed as
the difference between the priced cumulative volume through each day and through the previous day
within the same month — volume-then-price at day granularity, never a scaled priced figure (D-06).
Nothing on this page divides a priced figure by a day count.

**The dashed series starts at `as_of_day`, not after it (planner decision).** 07-UI-SPEC E2 requires
the solid line to meet the dashed line at today's data point; with `connectNulls={false}` on both
series they only join if they share one x-value. So the projected series carries the actual value
at `as_of_day` and the projected values thereafter. Days before `as_of_day` that the RPC flagged as
inferred are deliberately NOT drawn dashed — a dashed spur in the middle of history would read as a
rendering fault; the inferred-day count is stated in the method caption instead (D-03's honesty
lives in the caption, per 07-CONTEXT §specifics).

**The D-12 gate is applied in the Server Component, not inside the chart (07-PATTERNS binding).**
The chart component receives data that is already correct for the scope; it does not decide whether
to draw a projection.

**The projected card is not drillable, and this resolves an open 07-CONTEXT question.** A projection
has no contributing rows — some of the days it rests on have not happened yet — so a drill
affordance would lead somewhere empty or misleading. 07-UI-SPEC's Interaction contract declines it
explicitly and 07-CONTEXT's Deferred Ideas already carries it as the fallback if declined. Do not
add a `DrillableMetric` wrapper to the projected card and do not extend `DRILL_ENTITIES`.

**Scope boundary repeated from 07-03:** TSYS's secondary revenue figure on this page is not
drillable this phase either (07-UI-SPEC §"Planner consequences").
</planner_notes>
