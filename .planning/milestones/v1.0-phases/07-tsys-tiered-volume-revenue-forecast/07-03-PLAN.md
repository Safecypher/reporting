<objective>
Make the per-source revenue difference legible where it matters: give `/revenue`'s actual card its
variance phrase, add **Revenue** as a fifth paired metric card on `/alignment` whose status is
derived from the transaction volume card, and write the all-verifications billable-basis
explanation once so both surfaces render the same sentence.

Purpose: ROADMAP SC2 (FCST-02) — a revenue divergence is precisely what `/alignment` exists to
catch, and Phase 6 deferred this metric to Phase 7 by name (06 D-18). D-19's caption is the answer
to "why does our revenue figure exceed the TSYS invoice", moved out of `.planning` and onto the
dashboard where a reader can actually find it.

Output: a shared `SourceDeltaPhrase` component, a shared billable-basis caption module, a currency
formatting mode on `PairedMetricCard`, and a fifth card on `/alignment`.

**User story (surfaced, not invented):** see 07-01-PLAN.md §objective — the ROADMAP Phase 7 Goal
line is not in `As a / I want to / so that` form and no story has been invented in its place.
</objective>

<planner_notes>
**Scope boundary carried forward from 07-UI-SPEC.md §"Planner consequences" (recorded, not
silently assumed):** TSYS's secondary revenue figure on `/revenue` is **not drillable this phase**.
TSYS revenue rows come from `apigee_calls`, a table with no existing drill entity, and adding one
was judged out of scope for the KPI-pair work this phase asks for. A future phase wanting TSYS-side
revenue drill-down needs its own `DrillEntity` and fetcher, following the
`alignment-volume`/`alignment-live-cards` precedent in `lib/dashboard/drill-params.ts`. The Bit
Addict headline on `/revenue` stays drillable exactly as today.

**Divergence from 07-PATTERNS.md, deliberate:** PATTERNS proposes
`lib/dashboard/revenue-basis-caption.ts` as a plain exported string. The Copywriting Contract
requires the word "Reconciliation" inside that sentence to link to `/reconciliation`, which needs
JSX. The module therefore lives at `components/dashboard/revenue-basis-caption.tsx` and exports
BOTH the plain string (for tests and for any non-JSX consumer) and the rendered component, so there
is still exactly one copy of the wording.

**No new `ReconciliationStatus` value and no new tolerance.** The Revenue card's badge is the
volume card's already-resolved `AlignmentStatus`, passed through the same
`alignmentStatusToReconciliationStatus`/`alignmentStatusToLabel` helpers every other card uses
(D-11). The Phase 6 tolerance setting is not extended and gains no money equivalent.
</planner_notes>
